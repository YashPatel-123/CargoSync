Trip Lifecycle Workflow

1. Draft: Trip is created but vehicle/driver not assigned.
2. Dispatched: Vehicle & Driver assigned, goods in transit.
3. Completed: Goods delivered successfully.
4. Cancelled: Trip aborted (Validation: CargoWeight must not exceed MaxCapacity).
